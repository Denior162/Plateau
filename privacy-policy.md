# Privacy Policy for Plateau

**Effective Date:** November 23, 2026

Denys Zghonnyk ("we", "our", or "us") built the **Plateau** application as a commercial/personal utility app. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the Plateau mobile application.

## 1. Data Collection and Storage
**Plateau operates completely offline.** We do not collect, transmit, store, or process any personal or sensitive user data on external servers.

All data you enter or generate within the app—including plant names, botanical species, gravimetric weight measurements, watering history, and plant photographs—is stored **exclusively locally on your device** within the application's private internal storage (`filesDir` and local SQLite database).

## 2. Permissions and Device Access
* **Photos and Media (Photo Picker):** Plateau allows you to select images from your device gallery to attach to your plant profiles. Selected images are copied strictly into the app's private local directory (`filesDir`) for display within the app and are never uploaded or shared externally.
* **No Internet Transmission:** The app does not require an internet connection to function and does not transmit telemetry, crash analytics, or advertising identifiers.

## 3. Data Sharing and Third Parties
Because we do not collect any user data, we do not sell, trade, or share your information with any third parties, advertisers, or analytics providers.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion
You retain full control over your data. You can delete individual plant records, measurements, or photos directly within the app at any time. Uninstalling the Plateau application from your device permanently deletes all locally stored database records and associated image files.

## 5. Children's Privacy
Plateau does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
